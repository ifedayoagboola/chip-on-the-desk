The chooser only shows devices advertising the Immediate Alert service (0x1802). One device per pick, a user gesture every time, HTTPS or localhost only.

2. Immediate Alert (write without response)

Writes one byte to characteristic 0x2A06. On the DK6 board: mild turns LED2 on, high makes LED2 flash, no alert turns it off.

Link Loss level (write with response)

current: –

The chip keeps this. When the link drops, the chip itself raises the alert: mild flashes the blue LED, high flashes the green one. The browser only finds out through gattserverdisconnected.

3. Battery (read + notify)

–

The firmware reports a fixed 90%. The point is the shape: one read, then startNotifications and a characteristicvaluechanged listener.

4. The GATT queue

Unqueued, concurrent GATT calls race the one radio link and Chrome rejects with NetworkError: GATT operation already in progress. Queued, they run one after another and all succeed.

Exponential backoff: 1s, 2s, 4s, 8s, 16s, then give up. Every connect() has a 10 second timeout, because a hung connect also hangs the next requestDevice().
